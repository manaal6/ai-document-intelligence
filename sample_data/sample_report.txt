EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This report covers the second quarter of 2026 for TechNova Solutions. Revenue increased by 23% compared to Q1 2026, driven primarily by the launch of our AI-powered analytics platform. The company onboarded 150 new enterprise clients and expanded operations into three new markets: Southeast Asia, Latin America, and Eastern Europe.

KEY FINDINGS

1. Total revenue reached $4.2 million, exceeding the quarterly target of $3.8 million by 10.5%.

4. The AI analytics platform processed over 12 million data queries during the quarter.

5. Customer satisfaction score (CSAT) improved to 4.7 out of 5.0.
